## 0. Verdict

**CONCERNS.** The corpus is internally well-structured (the 2026-07-08 "corpus maturity" pass left good bones: mapping tables, honesty overlays, pin tests) but it has fallen **~700 commits behind the code it governs**, and the drift is now of three kinds:

The August "headless slice" wave (DRG-179, 196, 206–230: approval/escalation gates, authority disposition, targetability chain, custody, provenance, identity, CDE, threat/weapon recommendation, scarcity ranking, C2 nodes/packages/network health, task-group gaps, mission-command intent, after-action ledger, declutter, degrade), the S108–S116 C2 picture (CMD-31…39, auto-pause, toasts), air/boat ops, campaign/scenario library, Platform Design Assistant, ordnance bands, and the QA gauntlet are all invisible or marked "Open/Phase N" in the docs.
